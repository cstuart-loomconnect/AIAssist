AI Assist Settings

Org-wide behaviour and appearance settings, with Org-wide, Profile and User levels.

Behaviour worth knowing

  • Only five limits can be overridden below org level: message length, messages per minute and three tool-calling numbers.
  • Editing requires the AIAssistManageSettings custom permission.
